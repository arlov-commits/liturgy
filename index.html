    Print

    Perfect bound (Settings → Binding): 4 booklet pages on each side of a letter sheet.

    Printing on letter paper

    1. In the print window: choose your printer (or Save as PDF), paper Letter, print on both sides → flip on long edge, scale 100% / “Default” (not “fit”).
    2. Cut each sheet into four: across the middle, then down the middle.
    3. Stack the pieces in page order — each piece has an odd page on one side and the next even page on the other: 1–2, 3–4, 5–6, 7–8, then the next sheet's 9–10 … Ready to glue.

    Page order on each sheet — front: 2 3 / 6 7, back: 4 1 / 8 5 (then 9–16 on the next sheet). The front of each half is a pair of facing pages. Blank pages fill up the last sheet.

    Perfect bound, pages in order (Settings → Binding): each booklet page on its own sheet of paper, in reading order — nothing is rearranged.

    Printing

    1. In the print window: paper the size of the pages (or Save as PDF for a print shop), print on both sides → flip on long edge, scale 100% / “Default” (not “fit”).
    2. Each sheet then has an odd page on the front and the next even page on the back. Trim if needed, stack in order and glue at the spine.

    Signatures (Settings → Binding): letter sheets printed sideways, two pages a side, folded in half.

    Printing and folding

    1. In the print window: paper Letter, landscape, print on both sides → flip on short edge, scale 100% / “Default” (not “fit”).
    2. Fold each sheet in half, the printed page 1 side outwards.
    3. For each signature, nest its sheets in the order they came out of the printer: the first one is the outside, each next one goes inside it. Then the next signature, and so on.
    4. Stack the signatures in order and sew, staple or glue them at the fold.
